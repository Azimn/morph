# M.O.R.P.H. in the digital-creature research lineage

M.O.R.P.H. belongs to an existing developmental research line. Its specific contribution so far is inspectable software-organ allocation with genuine absence before birth, explicit affordability, deletion after atrophy, and receipts for those transitions. The broader idea that identical artificial founders can develop differently under different histories is already present in Vivarium, JELLY, Kurzweil, Creature Lab, and Bicentennial Man. The comparison does not establish external scientific novelty or a common performance ranking.

This review read current repository sources and the original Vivarium archive. Exact commits, file hashes, and evidence roles are preserved in [the source manifest](COMPARISON_SOURCE_PINS.json), with clickable [source references](COMPARISON_REFERENCES.md). Older experiments were not rerun. Vivarium's published means were recomputed from its twelve raw paired records and matched the archived aggregate. Secondary journal summaries are explicitly weaker evidence than source reports.

## Vivarium Creature Engine

The original LLM-free engine is the strongest nearby example of learned behavioral divergence under a frozen adult comparison. Its twelve same-genome paired histories developed in east-biased versus west-biased resource worlds, then froze learning and reset location and viability before matched neutral evaluation. Mean learned controller-weight distance was 0.092879, versus zero with plasticity disabled. Mean neutral action-distribution distance was 0.097833 with plasticity versus 0.027958 without. Residue erasure also affected immediate output and changed longer trajectories in five of eight replications, with a modest long-horizon effect.

This is stronger than MORPH-001 on replicated learned behavior and neutral transfer. It tests continuous controller plasticity inside an existing neural/body architecture, whereas M.O.R.P.H. tests whether an entire processing structure is present at all. Vivarium's report explicitly leaves lifetime structural growth and pruning as a future gate. The appropriate inheritance is its freeze/reset/probe discipline and no-plasticity comparison, not its numerical effect size, which has different units.

## Kurzweil Brain Experiments

The v0.1 source report preserves 36 lives: one fixed 1,024-neuron founder, twelve paired experience seeds, three environments, and 540,000 developmental ticks. Canonical development exceeded the rich matched condition in eleven of twelve pairs, but the computer-access ablation exceeded canonical in all twelve. Its conclusion was developmental differentiation in the simulator, not reconstruction of a particular person's developmental attractor.

The v0.5 branch report is more directly relevant to the next M.O.R.P.H. question. A matched intervention caused immediate contextual-value divergence in all twelve pairs, but most reconverged strongly. Median value distance fell from 0.091019 immediately after intervention to 0.00002355 at maturity; action-frequency divergence survived in only one of twelve mature pairs. The report retains independent post-run review as pending. The examined v0.6 head contains a timing protocol and integrity tests; this review does not treat that as an executed v0.6 outcome.

MORPH-001 reproduces the general shape of environment-contingent differentiation at a software-module level. It does not surpass Kurzweil's evidence for learning or persistence. The most useful next inheritance is the v0.6 atomic event swap: preserve content, opportunities, and total time, then distinguish immediate changes from lasting behavior under identical continuation.

## JELLY Jar and Native Jar

The journal's historical summary reports JELLY lesion survival and developmental anatomical divergence, while explicitly noting weaker later functional divergence. That is a close ancestor of M.O.R.P.H.'s structural question: history can alter internal organization without establishing a corresponding behavioral advantage. The original JELLY run artifacts were not reverified in this review, so no numerical or replicated effect is newly asserted.

Native Jar proposed treating operating-system resources, processes, files, IPC, scheduler time, and memory as organism physics. M.O.R.P.H.'s reserve is a synthetic ledger with actual allocation consequences inside the harness, not measurement of those physical resources. The comparison therefore identifies conceptual continuity, not completion of Native Jar's hypothesis.

## Creature Lab and PEMA-Creaturez

The journal identifies Creature Lab v0.1 as a long-history information-asymmetry/resource-competition baseline and v0.2 as bounded ontogenetic plasticity, with PEMA-Creaturez becoming the canonical project. The accessible current PEMA-Creaturez main branch is a README stub. This review could not substantiate the more detailed historical v0.3 quantitative claims from that public head and does not promote recalled numbers into fresh source-verified results.

This lineage already asks whether lived experience changes future processing rather than merely adding records. M.O.R.P.H. is useful as a small auditable mechanism test for that question. It remains a resource-aware allocator over authored structures until stronger evidence distinguishes it from that simpler description. Its 1:1 pressure-to-template mapping still encodes the developmental destination in advance.

## Bicentennial Man and the DUCK founder

The preserved founder characterization contains six lifetimes and 96 interactions. It records three observed actions and exactly three corresponding deterministic response strings. Identical prompts sometimes produced different actions after different scenario orders, but order also changed elapsed simulated time. The report explicitly does not isolate social-history effects from age or other time-dependent state.

Bicentennial Man's current design calls for matched-history replication, identical terminal batteries, surface-mutability controls, and held-out transfer before treating a score gain as cognitive improvement. Its planned 20-clone, 100,000-tick accelerated-life design is not treated here as a newly verified completed result. M.O.R.P.H. should inherit the matched-age battery and replication gates, while preserving the distinction between internal action changes and surface-language variation.

## Pretorius neural plasticity and recall

Pretorius 001F exposed six paired seeds to 450 reconstructed autobiographical records and changed recurrent weights, but chronological, reverse, and shuffled exposure had negligible differences on the old exposed phenotype metric. Plastic chronological replay slightly reduced that old score by about 0.000236. This establishes weight change without useful demonstrated order-sensitive autobiographical phenotype.

001G then tested a more targeted readout and found exactly zero plastic-minus-frozen top-1 improvement in every seed. Neural top-1 was about 5.48%, while raw lexical encoder similarity reached 10%. The 99.33% BC01 cue-index roundtrip used privileged cue metadata also used to construct queries, so its source report explicitly disallows presenting that as a fair architecture contest. Those percentages cannot rank M.O.R.P.H.'s exact-key store against neural or semantic recall.

The inheritance is methodological: content-state changes and self-description are insufficient. Require a functional readout, a mechanism intervention, wrong/absent-memory controls, and clear identification of external stored information. M.O.R.P.H.'s recollection is explicit external record storage, not an implicit learned neural autobiography.

## What the comparison changes

The next assay is [MORPH-002](protocols/MORPH-002-matched-event-order.md), frozen locally before new outcomes were inspected. It reuses the inherited runtime without retuning lifecycle parameters and compares the same complete event multiset in different orders at identical ages. It separates transient topology from retained information, uses an identical continuation, tests on nonmutating copies, and removes the memory organ to check joint necessity for recall.

A preallocated-memory control asks whether the order gap requires birth timing rather than merely differently ordered failures. Its inherited pruning and regrowth remain active, so it is not a permanently fixed-topology control. The protocol's phrase fixed-memory names availability from turn zero, not an invariant lifetime architecture. That control-label limitation is explicitly retained in the [result report](MORPH-002-RESULTS.md). A genuinely pinned, cost-matched whole-architecture comparator remains future work.

The closest overall ancestor is the Kurzweil/JELLY developmental lane. Vivarium supplies the strongest relevant learning/ablation discipline, Bicentennial Man supplies the equal-age and matched-history safeguards, and Pretorius supplies the warning against mistaking measurable internal change for functional memory. M.O.R.P.H. adds an unusually inspectable conditional-allocation mechanism to that lineage; broader developmental advantage remains unestablished.
