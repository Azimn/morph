# Articles V3 qualification campaign

The target is a mature M.O.R.P.H. configuration and the separate unified Pretorius candidate with evidence supporting each of the 34 specified capacity claims. Neither candidate has reached that target. Qualification is tied to a particular deployment and claim, not a global consciousness score, and no universal final form is assumed by the source framework.

## Fixed source

Its complete 34 Article passages and both tables were read. Every heading and passage hash matches the existing Pretorius source contract. The [machine-readable work inventory](ARTICLES-V3-CAMPAIGN.json) names every claim, its required controls, current mechanisms, and missing work for both candidates.

The evidence evaluator is reused from The-Doctor-Lives `fb236f1`, not a new qualification authority. Engineering outcomes remain separate from Supported, Unsupported, and Unassessable Article outcomes. A packet must bind an actual registered claim to source and protocol hashes, prerequisite availability, discriminating opportunities, controls, and observations. The evaluator checks supplied records; it does not authenticate researchers, establish test independence, or detect fabricated measurements. The source's three history sessions, two delayed opportunities, and one transfer context remain a proposed starting convention, not validated pass thresholds.

## Development sequence

The first work wave closes the decorative-state gap: consequential memory, constrained decisions, learned tool affordances, and intervention/restoration affecting executed outcomes. MORPH-003 is the first new engineering result in that wave. Pretorius separately gains opt-in native-policy task execution without an alternative brain or renderer choosing the action. No production Pretorius merge or standalone-organ replacement is made.

The next mechanism wave needs partner-specific belief and intention predictions, acquired procedural behavior with actual execution-cost measurement, anticipation before a deadline, context-conditioned affordance adaptation, and benign consequence-contingent future operating options. Existing fact sets, macro flags, deadline queues, and synthetic taxes do not by themselves satisfy those claims. Social, humor, and cultural claims require differences in later behavior, not merely stored labels.

The longitudinal wave needs independently authored discriminating tasks, meaningful separated opportunities, same-information and language-only baselines, task-relevant sequence counterfactuals, source-specific interventions, and held-out transfer. Developmental claims should be followed across repeated opportunities over days or weeks as appropriate. Fast synthetic sessions cannot stand in for that duration without disclosure. A zygote's appropriate absence of adult organs is a prerequisite stopping condition, not a failure of its eventual mature configuration.

The presentation and access wave needs live-model paraphrase and renderer substitutions, independent unsupported-claim checking, history-sensitive reflection contrasted with convergent profundity, advance predictions rather than only retrospective traces, controlled reveal/withhold calibration, recognition-based identity-marker continuity, and consented reset/deletion behavior. Current deterministic formatting and identity names alone do not establish these.

## Completion condition

The campaign completes only when each claimed capacity has a source-grounded protocol and adequate deployment evidence that survives its relevant alternatives. A genuine opportunity with adverse evidence is Unsupported, and missing prerequisites close the probe as Unassessable. Failed studies are preserved. A module installation, a large unit-test count, a favorable synthetic choice, or a fluent self-report cannot close an Article. The source framework itself remains conceptual and unvalidated; even complete local functional support would not establish phenomenal consciousness or universal validity.

Test fixtures are not study submissions.
